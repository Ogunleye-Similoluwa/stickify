# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Stickify to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, nationality, appearance, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation or disability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give and receive feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and help newcomers get started.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that don't follow this code, and may block people who repeatedly ignore it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled confidentially.
