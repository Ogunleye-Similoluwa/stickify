Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Stickify turns GIFs, videos and images into WhatsApp stickers. This page explains what happens to your data.

Making stickers

Stickers are made in your browser, on your device. The files you upload or the media from X posts you paste stay on your device. If your browser can't make the sticker itself, the file is sent to our server, converted, and deleted straight away.

Looking up X posts

When you paste a link to an X post, your browser asks the public fxtwitter service (and, as a backup, our server) for the media in that post. We don't store the links you paste.

Sending stickers on WhatsApp

When you tap Send to WhatsApp, the finished sticker is kept on our server for up to 30 minutes under a short code. When you send that code to the Stickify WhatsApp number, we reply with the sticker. To do this, WhatsApp (Meta) shares your phone number and your message with us. We use them only to send your sticker back, and we don't store them after replying. Stickers that aren't collected are deleted after 30 minutes.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no tracking cookies and no advertising.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We don't send you messages you didn't ask for.

Services we rely on

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages and Netlify, our server runs on Render, and WhatsApp messages go through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Each of them handles basic technical data such as your IP address to deliver their service.

Deleting your data

We don't keep personal data after your sticker is sent. If you have questions, or want us to confirm nothing is stored, email simiogunleye18@gmail.com.

Your content

Only convert media you have the right to use.
