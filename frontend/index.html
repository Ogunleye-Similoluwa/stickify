Download sticker

Pick WhatsApp in the share menu, then choose a chat. If it arrives as a photo instead of a sticker, download it and use the steps below.

Getting it into WhatsApp

  1. On a computer

    Open WhatsApp Web or the desktop app, open any chat (a chat with yourself works), and drag the downloaded .webp file in. It sends as a sticker.

  2. Save it on your phone

    Open that chat on your phone, tap the sticker, and choose Add to Favorites. It's now in your sticker tray.

  3. Phone only?

    Save the file, then import it with a sticker-pack app such as Sticker.ly. WhatsApp needs at least three stickers in a pack.

Only convert media you have the right to use. Files are processed in memory and deleted right after conversion.
